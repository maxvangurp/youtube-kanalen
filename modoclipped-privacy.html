Privacy Policy – Modoclipped

Modoclipped is a private tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @modoclipped. It uses the TikTok API to publish the owner's own videos and to read basic profile information and video statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) of that same account.

No data of other TikTok users is collected. The data stays on the owner's own computer, is not shared with third parties and is not used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services.
